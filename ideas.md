# Design Exploration — Dr. Ketan Desai | Shreeya Hospital

## Three stylistic approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Garden Rounds | A calm, editorial healthcare identity shaped by botanical forms, warm paper tones, and restrained clinical clarity. It translates compassionate pediatric care into a human, reassuring visual rhythm. | 0.07 |
| Clear Horizon | A bright, contemporary medical studio with pale blues, modular information cards, and a technical-but-friendly cadence. It would foreground efficiency and access. | 0.04 |
| Family Atelier | A tactile, art-directed family-health journal using playful hand-drawn details and sun-washed color. It would feel expressive and community-minded. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach — Garden Rounds

### Design Movement

**Editorial modernism with biophilic healthcare cues.** The design borrows the reference image’s confident, asymmetric typography and magazine-like information hierarchy, then translates its dark green restraint into a warm pediatric-care setting.

### Core Principles

1. **Composed asymmetry:** generous blank space carries equal importance to imagery, creating calm and credibility.
2. **Human reassurance:** information is direct and sparse, never clinical or cold; each section resolves into a practical next step.
3. **Botanical care language:** soft arcs, seed dots, and leaf-like strokes signal growth without resorting to childish illustration.
4. **Tactile precision:** fine rules, paper-like surfaces, and intentional type scale replace generic cards and excessive rounded forms.

### Color Philosophy

The page begins in **warm porcelain** to feel human and familiar rather than institutional. **Deep forest green** communicates competence, stability, and nature-led growth; **eucalyptus sage** makes secondary information feel quieter. A single **coral seed** accent is reserved for moments of action and human warmth, creating a visual pulse without compromising the clinical trust of the page.

### Layout Paradigm

The site is arranged as an **editorial field guide** rather than a centered marketing page: an oversized statement sits in a generous left column, while the doctor’s portrait occupies an anchored right panel. Subsequent content alternates between narrow annotation rails, horizontal rules, full-bleed color bands, and off-axis image blocks. Mobile collapses this logic into a clear vertical narrative while retaining the sectional sequence.

### Signature Elements

1. **The compass seed:** small coral dots framed by thin green circles mark key moments and actions.
2. **Growth lines:** hairline rules capped by leaf-like arcs structure reading and connect otherwise open areas.
3. **Portrait frame:** a forest-green inset image field with a discrete caption band gives the doctor’s photo editorial presence.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should reassure rather than entertain. CTAs are outlined or filled tokens with clear verbs; their hover state translates a coral seed dot into an arrow. Navigation underlines grow from left to right, linking interaction to the site’s growth motif.

### Animation

Page elements use a single gentle upward fade-in at load, staggered in short increments. Rules grow horizontally, and image frames subtly settle into place. Hover effects are under 200ms with a precise, quiet ease-out. Respect reduced-motion preferences by removing non-essential transformations.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** is the editorial voice for statements and select section labels; **Manrope** is the calm, highly legible system for navigation, body content, and actions. Headlines use tight tracking, line-height near 0.94, and a dynamic scale. Body copy remains compact with generous leading. Uppercase labels use Manrope at small sizes and wider tracking.

### Brand Essence

**Shreeya Hospital helps Ghatlodia families move from childhood concerns to a healthy, confident future through compassionate, doctor-led care.**

Personality: **reassuring, attentive, grounded.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise, hopeful, and calm. CTAs state the next practical action plainly. Microcopy acknowledges family needs without generic hospital phrasing.

Examples:

> “Small concerns deserve thoughtful attention.”

> “Speak with Dr. Ketan Desai.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is set as a custom-feeling, lower-case-inspired word pairing where the brand name is compact and the hospital descriptor sits on a second, quieter line. Its symbol is a continuous protective heart that resolves into a sprout and forward path—an abstract promise of care and growth.

### Signature Brand Color

**Shreeya Forest — #1F4B3A.** A grounded deep green that remains calm on ivory surfaces and ownable in a local healthcare context.

## Style Decisions

- Dr. Ketan’s photograph is always held inside a **Shreeya Forest editorial portrait frame**, with a forest field, tonal grading, and a caption/stamp treatment; it is never presented as a plain clinical image on white.
- The protective heart-sprout mark is the **primary recurring signature**, visible in the header and footer, section indexes, portrait stamp, and compact compass details.
- Supporting visuals use a **paper-textured botanical editorial language** in forest, sage, porcelain, and restrained coral, so they sit naturally with the portrait treatment.
- Coral remains a disciplined navigation cue: it marks the seed dot, key action focus, and attention moments without becoming a decorative fill color.
